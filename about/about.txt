Kairo turns your voice or text into perfect AI prompts and remembers you across every AI tool — forever.

\The Problem:
Every day 500 million people use free AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude. They have two painful problems:

They know what they want but can't explain it to AI properly so they get bad results
Every new chat they have to re-explain who they are and what they need from scratch — wasting hours every week


The Solution:
Kairo is a voice-to-prompt AI app with two superpowers:
Superpower 1 — Prompt Builder
Speak or type your rough idea in plain language. Kairo instantly converts it into a perfectly structured AI prompt that gets you 10x better results from any AI tool.
Superpower 2 — AI Passport
Build your personal AI identity once. Your goals, your context, your style. One tap generates your context block — paste it into ANY free AI tool instantly. No more re-explaining yourself ever again.
