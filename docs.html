How to get the most out of Mindwave.

The AI Passport

Your AI Passport is a compressed representation of your identity, goals, and communication style. It is designed to be prepended to any LLM prompt (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) to establish immediate context.

  • Keep your context updated as your projects change.
  • Use different Memory Modes to switch between different contexts (e.g. Founder vs Developer).
  • Toggle the passport injection before copying your final prompt.

Voice Capture

Speak naturally. We transcribe your raw thoughts and use an AI waterfall to generate a perfect prompt. You don't need to format your speech; the AI knows how to structure it based on your selected target (e.g., Claude vs. ChatGPT).

Session Sync

When you reach the context window limit in an AI chat, copy the entire conversation and paste it into Session Sync. We will generate a compressed continuity block that you can paste into a fresh chat to resume exactly where you left off.

API Access (Ultra Tier)

Ultra tier users can access the Mindwave API to programmatically generate prompts and passports. Contact support for API keys.
