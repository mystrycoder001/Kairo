Frequently asked questions

An AI Passport is a structured identity profile created by Cloasta that captures your name, role, communication style, active projects, behavioral preferences, and AI optimization targets. It is designed to be prepended to AI prompts so any AI tool instantly understands who you are.

Your AI Passport is a text-based identity block attached to your prompts. When you generate a prompt in Cloasta, the passport is automatically included. You can then paste the complete prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any AI platform.

The AI Passport has 6 layers: Identity (name, role), Communication style (tone, format, style), Active context (current projects), Behavioral memory (work habits, tools), Permanent context (critical info), and AI optimization target (which platform to optimize for).

On the free plan you get 1 AI Passport. The Pro plan ($7.99/month) includes 5 AI Passports. Ultra ($15.99/month) offers unlimited passports.
