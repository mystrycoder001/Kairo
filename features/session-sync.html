The problem: context window limits

Every AI tool has a context window limit. When conversations get long, the AI starts forgetting what you discussed earlier. You lose progress, decisions, and important context.

Starting a new chat means starting from zero — unless you have a way to carry your conversation context forward.

How Session Sync works
