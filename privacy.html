Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Mindwave ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This policy describes the types of information we may collect from you or that you may provide when you visit the website Mindwave.app and our practices for collecting, using, maintaining, protecting, and disclosing that information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, and role as provided during onboarding.
  • Voice Data: Audio recordings are processed in real-time via Groq Whisper API. We do not store audio files on our servers.
  • AI Contexts: Your Identity Passports, session sync histories, and generated prompts.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to generate tailored AI prompts, maintain your Memory Modes, provide customer support, and improve the app's performance via Gemini and OpenRouter integrations.

4. GDPR Compliance

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights. We aim to take reasonable steps to allow you to correct, amend, delete, or limit the use of your Personal Data.

5. Subscriptions & Payments

Mindwave operates a Free-forever model alongside paid Pro and Ultra subscriptions. Payment processing is handled securely via Razorpay. We do not store your full payment details.
